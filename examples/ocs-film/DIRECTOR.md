# OCS — a message crosses the gap

Subject: cross-session messaging and waking agents. Source: the current Open Cross-session README, checked 2026-10-07. Supported names, direct messages and local/paired LAN operation are factual. The moving packets and UI are an illustration, not a recording or fabricated terminal output.

Borrowed principle from zero's UI studies on X: a persistent object transforms and carries the eye between states. This film does not copy their code, artwork or music. Reference: https://x.com/twoclipping/status/2103835273813496100

14.4 seconds, 100 BPM, 24 beats. One visual motif: a coral dot becomes a message, travels to a green recipient, expands into a wake pulse, becomes a network, then resolves into the wordmark. Warm paper, oversized dark typography, coral/green agent identities. Designed framing for landscape and portrait; all visible names and copy are supplied in Chinese and English.

Four key frames: opposing agent names; opened message; frame-filling wake pulse; network resolving to the product name. The camera follows the action rather than keeping a title above a diagram. Each shot has Chinese and English narration, generated and cached independently; the picture is retimed to fit complete utterances. Original synthesized music uses the same BPM as the animation. Four CC0/original bundled effects accompany send, arrival, network reveal and wordmark. Their start times subtract the measured transient offsets (click 0.21688s, whoosh 0.07757s, switch 0.12142s, ding 0.00694s).
