# Kinetic — hold, push, slam

Takeaway: the pulse rhythm in three words — hold, move fast on the event, hold — and that it makes a film, not a slideshow.

Look: kinetic-type poster, `poster` palette, one heavy word per beat, a navy chapter for SLAM, nothing moves after it lands. Built from `M.stamp`, `M.words`, `M.caption`, `M.stroke`, `M.shoot` with push, slam and one shake; chiptune music with hits on each word.

Beats: HOLD slams and stays still → the camera pushes 28% onto PUSH in 0.28 s → SLAM crashes in on navy and the frame shakes once → "Not a slideshow" rises word by word and a red stroke crosses out the last word (portrait sets it on two lines so the stroke lands on the right word).

Measured (draft renders): fast_ratio 0.043 en / 0.021 zh — yellow, accepted only because the film is 12 s. Long stretches of a single still word are the point of this look; a longer kinetic film at this pace would fail the pacing gate and should add moves or cut holds. The Chinese words are single characters, so even less of the frame changes. Not watched end to end; music not listened to.
